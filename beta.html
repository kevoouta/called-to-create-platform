Beta Tester FAQ

A Few Things to Know

The beta is a chance to help us learn before we build further. Here is what taking part means.

Why are you running a beta? +

We want to learn from real creatives before the platform grows. Testing helps us find confusing flows, spot issues, and prioritize the courses and tools that are most valuable to the community.

Who can sign up? +

Any curious creative, freelancer, or entrepreneur is welcome to register interest. You do not need a particular background or technical experience.

What will I be asked to do? +

If selected, you may be invited to explore parts of the platform and share feedback about your experience. We will explain any testing activities when we reach out.

Does signing up guarantee a place? +

Signing up lets us know you are interested. Our team will contact you with next steps and availability; registration itself does not guarantee immediate access.

How will you use my details? +

We will use your contact details to follow up about beta testing. We will not publish them. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
