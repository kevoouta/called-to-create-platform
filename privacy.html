Data & Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

We respect user privacy and only collect personal information essential to facilitating your creative learning experience:

  • Account Identity: Your full name and email address provided during account creation.
  • Transaction Records: Billing details associated with Paystack (M-Pesa numbers, payment confirmations). We do not directly store full payment card numbers on our servers.
  • Platform Progress: Lesson completion metrics, saved asset downloads, and progress states within your account.

2. How Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to deliver class streaming, verify purchase ownership, deliver instant download links, and dispatch platform announcements (if opted into our newsletter dispatch). We never sell or share user data with third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We integrate securely with trusted services to fulfill our platform functionality:

  • Paystack: Payment gateway handling card processing and M-Pesa checkout workflows.
  • Analytics & Infrastructure: Internal telemetry used exclusively to monitor video playback stability and platform uptime.

4. Your Rights & Data Requests

You retain full rights to request an export of your personal information or request account deletion by emailing support@calledtocreate.com.
