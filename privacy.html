Data & Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We respect user privacy and only collect personal information essential to facilitating your creative learning experience:

  • Account Identity: Your full name and email address provided during account creation.
  • Transaction Records: Billing details associated with Paystack (M-Pesa numbers, payment confirmations). We do not directly store full payment card numbers on our servers.
  • Platform Progress: Lesson completion metrics, saved asset downloads, and progress states within your account.
  • Anonymous Site Metrics: Page paths and aggregated browser performance measurements (such as page-load timing and layout stability). These metrics are not linked to an account and do not use advertising cookies or persistent visitor IDs.

2. How Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to deliver course streaming, verify purchase ownership, deliver instant download links, and dispatch platform announcements (if opted into our newsletter dispatch). We never sell or share user data with third-party advertisers.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We integrate securely with trusted services to fulfill our platform functionality:

  • Paystack: Payment gateway handling card processing and M-Pesa checkout workflows.
  • Analytics & Infrastructure: Aggregated first-party page-usage, download-link, and browser-performance metrics used to improve the site, alongside service diagnostics from Supabase, Paystack, and Bunny Stream.

Site metrics omit account IDs, visitor IDs, and URL query strings. Page views and successful download-link issuances are aggregated by page and month; monthly summaries are retained for up to 12 months. A download-link metric records that a secure link was issued, not that a file transfer completed.

4. Your Rights & Data Requests

You retain full rights to request an export of your personal information or request account deletion by emailing support@calledtocreate.com.
