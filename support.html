Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my purchased classes and project files?

Once a payment is confirmed via Paystack (M-Pesa or Card), your purchased masterclass and associated downloadable files (.drx PowerGrades, templates) immediately unlock in your My Library dashboard.

Do I need to pay every month?

No. We do not use recurring subscriptions. You pay a single one-time price per class or asset pack and receive lifetime access.

What software version do I need for the DaVinci Resolve PowerGrades?

Our node tree PowerGrades and LUT collections are compatible with DaVinci Resolve 17, 18, and 19 (both Studio and Free versions).

My M-Pesa transaction went through but my lesson is locked. What should I do?

Occasionally network delays take up to 2 minutes to sync transaction webhooks. Refresh your page or submit a support inquiry using the contact form with your M-Pesa confirmation code.
